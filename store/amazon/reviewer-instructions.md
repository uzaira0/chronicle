# Amazon reviewer instructions

Test on physical Fire OS 6+ hardware without a Google account, Play Store, Play Services, or Health
Connect. Enroll with the reviewer study credentials supplied in Appstore Console, inspect module
status in Data Sharing, confirm the displayed public-HTTPS study server matches the review
instructions, reboot, and confirm supported collection resumes. Rotate between portrait
and landscape. Open Settings > Study server details > Withdraw from study and confirm collection
stops. Withdrawing sends a withdrawal request to the study server; data already collected is kept or
erased by the study team, according to this study's withdrawal policy.

Console-only evidence still required: Fire OS 6/7 and Fire OS 8 device packets, privacy-label form,
content rating, target audience, legal developer/support contacts, final screenshots, Live App
Testing, and Quality Central results.
