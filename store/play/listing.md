# Google Play listing

App name (30 characters maximum):

Chronicle Research

Short description (80 characters maximum):

Secure data collection for approved research studies.

Full description:

Chronicle is a reusable participant app for approved research studies. A device can have one active
study at a time. Before collection starts, Chronicle identifies the study and its public HTTPS
server, explains every requested data type, records the participant's choices, and does not begin
collection until consent is given. Participants can review Data Sharing, change optional choices,
or stop Chronicle collection by uninstalling the app.

FEATURES IN THIS RELEASE

- Exact-server enrollment using a study-issued invitation.
- Informed consent, study settings, and collection status.
- App-usage records: app package name and label, open and close timing, foreground duration, and an
  optional in-app activity or screen-class name. Chronicle does not read the content shown inside an
  app.
- Basic device telemetry: device lifecycle and reboot events; battery and charging state;
  connectivity type and state; and settings such as dark mode, font scale, Do Not Disturb, volume,
  brightness, free storage, thermal state, and whether location services are enabled. The
  location-services setting is not a location or coordinate.
- Optional unlock identification: after device unlock, the person using the device can indicate
  whether they are the study participant or someone else. Chronicle records only the selection and
  when it was made.
- Encrypted offline storage and authenticated upload to the exact enrolled study server. Bounded
  upload diagnostics record the affected upload family, failure category and count, timing, HTTP
  status when available, and bounded exception class. They do not include server URLs or free-form
  error text. They are
  uploaded after connectivity recovers and deleted from the device after acknowledgment or after
  30 days.

NOT COLLECTED AND NOT PLANNED

Chronicle does not collect and is not planned to collect app contents, typed text, messages,
notification content or metadata, accessibility or screen-interaction events, websites or search
terms, precise or approximate location, contacts, screenshots, photographs, video, microphone
recordings, audio or media metadata, advertising identifiers, financial information, or advertising
profiles. Chronicle contains no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK.

NOT IN THIS RELEASE, BUT POSSIBLE IN A FUTURE RELEASE

This release does not include Health Connect records, physical-activity or sleep recognition,
continuous hardware-sensor measurements, per-app network byte counts, or automatic crash reports.
These features are unavailable and collect nothing in this release. Any future addition would
require a separately reviewed app update, updated Play disclosures, explicit study configuration,
and participant consent before collection.

Research records are sent only to the server displayed during enrollment. Chronicle does not sell
participant data or use it for advertising or marketing. Each study's consent identifies its
responsible institution and research team and explains retention and disclosure practices.
Publishing Chronicle does not give the publisher access to another institution's study records.

To withdraw from Chronicle collection, uninstall the app. Uninstalling stops future collection and
Android removes Chronicle's on-device app data. The Play app has no in-app withdrawal or
server-deletion control, and uninstalling cannot notify the enrolled study server. Questions about
participation or records already uploaded must go to the research team identified in the study
consent.

Chronicle is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical
condition. Do not use Chronicle for medical decisions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional
for medical advice.
